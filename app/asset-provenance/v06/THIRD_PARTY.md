The `sources/<asset>/manifest.json` files retain exact source/download URLs, provider authorship, expected file sizes, MD5 and SHA-256 hashes. `source-files.json` retains provider metadata. Source files are preserved separately from modifications. No elephant asset or unverified Thai-temple prefab is used. No paid API, paid asset purchase or downloaded executable/script was used.
